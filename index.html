Say hello to the people who run TBB

A big howdy, Spring 2026

Spring is here and I'm loving this time of year. The days are getting longer, it's been incredibly sunny, and the gardens are coming alive. The hostel is buzzing with new travellers and a few familiar faces too.

When you arrive you'll be greeted by some of us, plus our amazing team of Exchange Workers. Each week we make soup and bread for a staff lunch together, and every night there's chocolate pudding for everyone.

We're very committed to the traveller community. Relationships and caring for each other come first here. If that sounds like you, come and stay. We'd love to have you.

Good to know before you come

We're not a flashpackers. Our walls are a bit thin and the furniture is second-hand. People come for the vibe, the friendships they make and the care they feel. One guest who stayed through lockdown in 2020 called it "maybe the first home you have ever really had".

Weeks, not nights

Stay a while. Plenty of people do.

A few weeks or a month here is a great way to start your time in Aotearoa, or to take a breather halfway through. Book a few nights first, see how you like us, then ask about weekly rates.

Work in Nelson, year round

There's usually plenty of work about, and we're happy to help you find it.

CafésAdminHorticultureThe portFishing companiesPaintingConstruction

Weekly rates for international travellers

We have a lovely mix of long-stay travellers working in Nelson and people passing through for a night or two. It keeps the place feeling like a community.

Ask at reception once you've settled in.

Digital nomads & students

In winter there's a dedicated space for online work and study. The rest of the year there are plenty of quiet corners. Ask about special rates for remote workers, mostly in the quieter months.

Nelson is a great place to spend the winter: markets, pottery classes, biking, hiking, saunas by the sea and yoga.

Right outside the door

Nelson is the sunny, arty corner of the South Island

Great coffee, craft beer, markets and makers in town. Golden beaches, the Abel Tasman and the mountains not far beyond. Winter or summer, there's loads to do.

On a budget?

Lucky you. So much in Nelson is free. So is our wifi, and so is the pudding.

  • Climb to the Centre of New Zealand for views over the city and out to Abel Tasman
  • Hire a bike and explore the beach, the river and town
  • Soak up the sun at Tahunanui Beach
  • Browse the Saturday Market and catch the buskers
  • The Suter Art Gallery is free on Saturdays
  • Beach volleyball with your new mates
  • Walk the Cable Bay Walkway, and plenty more
  • Visit local wineries and boutique breweries
